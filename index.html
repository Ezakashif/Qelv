Questions

Before you get started

Where does the rate data come from?

Rates are shopped from the public booking channels travellers use, captured with the room type, rate plan and restrictions attached so comparisons stay like-for-like.

How often is my comp set refreshed?

The standard cadence is four refreshes per day across the forward booking window. Higher-frequency monitoring is available on request.

What does the position index mean?

It compares your rate to the median of your comp set. An index of 100 is parity; above 100 you are priced over the market, below 100 you are priced under it.

How do I sign in?

Existing clients use the client login, which opens the secure portal over HTTPS. New accounts are provisioned by your account contact.
